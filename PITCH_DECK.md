## 🎯 1. The Executive Problem & Solution

### The Core Problem (The "Trust & Scheme Gap")
Every day in India, millions of citizens receive WhatsApp messages offering free laptops, student scholarships, or PM-Kisan payouts with a malicious `.apk` link attached.
- When citizens click: **They lose their life savings.**
- When citizens ignore: **They miss out on the genuine government welfare schemes they are legally entitled to.**
- When they are extorted ("Digital Arrest" or electricity cutoff threats): **They don't know who to call within the critical 2-hour Golden Hour before money is routed through mule accounts.**

### The Solution: JanRakshak AI
A unified, voice-first, multimodal AI Guardian that combines:
1. **Forensic Scam Shield Agent:** Detects malicious URLs, APK traps, urgency manipulation, and fake police notices.
2. **Civic Scheme Navigator Agent:** Converts fraudulent scheme traps into safe on-ramps for real, verified government portals (distilling 20-page bureaucratic circulars into 3 simple bullet points).
3. **1930 Golden-Hour Cyber Emergency Response Agent:** Auto-drafts formal Cybercrime Incident Reports in English ready for `cybercrime.gov.in` and provides immediate bank freeze checklists in Telugu, Hindi, and English.

## 🛡️ 4. Judge Q&A Defense Cheat Sheet

| Question | Winning Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Q: Why combine cyber fraud detection with government schemes?** | **A:** Because scammers almost exclusively weaponize real government schemes (PM-Kisan, Ladli Behna, Vidya Deevena) to lure victims. A standalone scam checker leaves users confused about whether the scheme is real; our platform verifies the real scheme and safely redirects them. |
| **Q: Why did you choose Gemini Flash over larger models?** | **A:** Real-world citizens need instant answers in emergency fraud situations. Gemini Flash provides sub-second latency, multimodal vision for screenshots, and robust adherence to structured JSON schemas at near-zero inference cost. |
| **Q: How do you prevent hallucinations in government scheme criteria?** | **A:** We use strict Pydantic schema enforcement with constrained temperature (0.2) and prompt boundaries that explicitly prohibit fabricated URLs, requiring official `.gov.in` or state portal domains. |
| **Q: What is the significance of the 1930 Golden Hour?** | **A:** In Indian cybercrime law, the first 2 hours after a fraudulent transaction are called the "Golden Hour". If reported to 1930 within this window, banks can freeze the suspect mule accounts before money is withdrawn at ATMs. Our auto-drafted report saves 30+ minutes of panicked typing. |
